Rotate logo

Drag the dots on the photo to position and shape the logo:
Purple dot — where the logo will be.
Blue dot — pull towards the right extent of the branding area.
Orange dot — pull towards the bottom extent of the branding area.
If the logo lands sideways, use the Rotate Logo button above instead of fighting the dots.

Pricing Sync

Pulls USD and CAD pricing straight from the distributor's own "Coded Pricelist" spreadsheet and updates the matching products here to match, so our numbers never drift out of sync with the real net pricelist. Matches by product code; a bundle/kit row that lists more than one SKU (e.g. the Chisel 4-pack) is skipped rather than guessed at, since a kit's price isn't any one component's real standalone price — those need a manual check. Always preview before applying — preview never changes anything, it just shows what would happen.
